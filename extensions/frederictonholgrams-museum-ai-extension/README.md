# FrederictonHolograms Museum and Community AI Learning Extension

This extension presents a Fredericton-first prototype for safe, accessible AI learning through holographic characters in museums, libraries, schools, tourism sites, and community spaces.

## Purpose

The extension helps cultural and community organizations explore AI literacy through clearly fictional characters, local content, staff-supported activities, and responsible governance practices.

It is designed to support visitor engagement, digital literacy, cultural interpretation, accessibility, and community participation without presenting AI as an unquestioned authority.

## Core concept

The proposed learning station uses fictional holographic characters to help visitors:

- Ask questions about local history, culture, science, and community topics
- Identify errors and verify sources
- Learn about privacy, consent, and responsible AI use
- Create responsibly with AI tools
- Access content through plain language, captions, large text, audio alternatives, and quiet-mode options

## Safety and governance

The extension follows a safety-first approach:

- All AI characters are clearly fictional
- Characters do not claim consciousness, humanity, or authority
- Important historical claims include source links or references
- Visitor creations require consent before saving or display
- No personal accounts are required
- Human staff escalation is available for concerns and accessibility needs
- The system does not provide medical, legal, or financial advice

## Pilot approach

The extension is intended as a small, measurable pilot. Potential measures include sessions completed, accessibility satisfaction, staff usefulness, content accuracy, consent compliance, and cost per session.
